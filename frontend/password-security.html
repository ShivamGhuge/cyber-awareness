Cybersecurity Topic

Password Security

Learn how to create, protect, and manage strong passwords to keep your online accounts secure.

Why Password Security Matters

Passwords are one of the main ways we protect our online accounts. A weak or reused password can make it easier for someone to gain unauthorized access.

What Makes a Strong Password?

1

Use Enough Length

Use a password that is long enough to make guessing more difficult.

2

Use Unique Passwords

Use different passwords for important accounts instead of reusing the same one.

3

Avoid Predictable Information

Avoid easily guessed information such as names, birthdays, or simple patterns.

4

Consider a Password Manager

A password manager can help you create and securely store unique passwords.

Practical Example

Imagine you use the same password for your email, social media, and college account. If that password becomes exposed, someone could try using it on your other accounts as well.

Using separate passwords for important accounts reduces the risk of one exposed password affecting multiple accounts.

Tips & Guidance

✓

Never Share Your Password

Do not share your password with friends, strangers, or people claiming to be support staff.

✓

Do Not Reuse Important Passwords

Keep important accounts protected with unique passwords.

✓

Be Careful With Password Requests

Do not enter your password into a website unless you are confident that the website is legitimate.

✓

Never Share OTPs or Security Codes

Keep one-time passwords and authentication codes private.
