Cybersecurity Topic

Privacy & Personal Data

Learn simple ways to protect your personal information when using websites, apps, and online services.

Key Safety Points

🔐

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing unnecessary information such as your address, phone number, identification details, or account information online.

👁️

Check Privacy Settings

Review privacy settings on social media, apps, and online accounts so you understand who can see your information.

📸

Think Before Posting

Consider who may see a photo, video, comment, or personal detail before posting it online.

📍

Be Careful With Location Data

Check which apps can access your location and disable access when it is not necessary.

📄

Check What You Are Sharing

Before submitting a form, check what information is being requested and whether it is necessary.

🗑️

Remove Unused Accounts

Consider closing old accounts that you no longer use and remove unnecessary personal information where possible.

Common Privacy Mistakes

❌

Oversharing on Social Media

Avoid publicly sharing information that could reveal your location, routine, contact details, or other sensitive information.

❌

Accepting Every Permission

Do not automatically allow apps access to contacts, camera, microphone, files, or location without checking why it is needed.

❌

Sharing Account Information

Never share passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, or authentication information with others.

❌

Posting Private Documents

Avoid uploading photos or documents that expose identification numbers, addresses, signatures, or other sensitive information.

Quick Example

You want to post a photo from a college event. Before posting it publicly, check whether the photo reveals your address, phone number, ID card, location, or other private information.

Do & Don't

✅

Do

Review privacy settings, limit unnecessary permissions, protect account information, and think before posting.

🚫

Don't

Do not publicly share sensitive information, accept unnecessary permissions, or share passwords and OTPs.

Quick Privacy Checklist

☑️

Before Posting

Check whether the content contains private information about you or someone else.

☑️

Before Installing an App

Check the permissions the app requests and decide whether they are necessary.

☑️

Before Sharing Information

Ask why the information is needed and whether you are comfortable sharing it.

Remember

  • Think before posting
  • Review privacy settings
  • Limit app permissions
  • Protect sensitive information
  • Never share passwords or OTPs

Quick Rule

If you would not want private information shared publicly, do not post or share it without checking who can access it.
