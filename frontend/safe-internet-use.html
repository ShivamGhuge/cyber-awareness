Cybersecurity Topic

Safe Internet Use

Simple points to help you browse, download, communicate, and use online services more safely.

Key Safety Points

🔗

Check Links Before Clicking

Check the website address before opening a link. Be careful with shortened or unexpected links.

🌐

Use Trusted Websites

Check the website address and avoid entering personal information on unfamiliar websites.

⬇️

Download Carefully

Download files and software only from trusted websites or official app stores.

💬

Be Careful With Online Messages

Do not trust messages simply because they appear to come from a friend, college, company, or service.

🔒

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, security codes, financial information, or unnecessary personal details online.

📶

Be Careful on Public Networks

Avoid sensitive activities on unknown public Wi-Fi networks and use trusted connections whenever possible.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌

Clicking Urgent Links

Do not click a link just because a message says you must act immediately.

❌

Using the Same Password Everywhere

Reusing an important password can put multiple accounts at risk if that password is exposed.

❌

Ignoring Browser Warnings

Do not ignore security warnings when visiting websites or downloading files.

❌

Sharing Sensitive Information

Never share passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, or other security information with strangers.

Quick Example

You receive a message saying that your college account will be disabled today and asking you to click a link to verify your account.

Instead of clicking the link, open the official college website yourself or contact the appropriate support team using a trusted contact method.

Do & Don't

✅

Do

Check website addresses, use trusted sources, update your software, and think before sharing information.

🚫

Don't

Do not click suspicious links, download unknown files, ignore security warnings, or share passwords and OTPs.

Quick Safety Checklist

☑️

Before Clicking

Check who sent the message and where the link will take you.

☑️

Before Downloading

Confirm that the file or application comes from a trusted source.

☑️

Before Sharing

Ask yourself whether the information is necessary to share.

Remember

  • Think before clicking
  • Check website addresses
  • Download from trusted sources
  • Protect personal information
  • Never share passwords or OTPs

Quick Rule

If something online feels unexpected, urgent, or suspicious, stop and verify it before taking action.
