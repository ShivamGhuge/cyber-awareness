Cybersecurity Topic

Phishing

Learn how to recognize suspicious messages, links, and websites designed to steal your information.

What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where someone uses a fake message, website, or communication to trick you into revealing information or performing an unsafe action.

Phishing messages can appear to come from trusted organizations, friends, colleges, banks, or online services.

How Phishing Works

1

A Suspicious Message Arrives

You may receive an email, text message, or social media message asking you to take an urgent action.

2

You Are Asked to Click a Link

The message may contain a link that claims to take you to a trusted website.

3

A Fake Website or Request Appears

The link may lead to a fake website designed to look like a legitimate service.

4

Information May Be Stolen

If you enter sensitive information, the attacker may attempt to misuse it.

Practical Example

Imagine receiving a message that appears to come from your college account service. It says that your account will be disabled unless you click a link and sign in immediately.

Instead of clicking the link, stop and verify the message through an official website or trusted contact.

Tips & Guidance

✓

Think Before Clicking

Do not immediately click links in unexpected messages.

✓

Check the Sender

Carefully check who sent the message and look for unusual details.

✓

Be Careful With Urgent Requests

Be cautious when a message pressures you to act immediately.

✓

Never Share Passwords or OTPs

Never provide passwords, OTPs, or authentication codes through suspicious messages or websites.

Remember

If a message makes you feel rushed or asks for sensitive information, stop and verify it before taking action.
