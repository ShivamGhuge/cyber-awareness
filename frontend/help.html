Need Help With a Cybersecurity Problem?

Find practical guidance for common online safety problems, learn what to do when something goes wrong, and report incidents through the appropriate channel.

QUICK HELP

What Do You Need Help With?

Find the situation closest to your problem and follow the recommended safety steps.

🔐

My Account Was Hacked

If someone may have accessed your account, act quickly to protect it.

  • Change your password immediately.
  • Sign out of unknown devices.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Check for suspicious account activity.
🎣

I Received a Suspicious Message

Be careful with unexpected messages, links, attachments, and requests.

  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Do not share passwords or OTPs.
  • Verify the sender independently.
  • Report suspicious activity.
🛑

Cyberbullying or Online Harassment

If someone is threatening, harassing, or repeatedly targeting you online.

  • Do not respond aggressively.
  • Save relevant evidence.
  • Block the person where appropriate.
  • Tell a trusted adult or authority.
📱

My Device Was Lost or Stolen

Protect your accounts and information if your phone, tablet, or computer is missing.

  • Use device location tools if available.
  • Lock the device remotely.
  • Change important account passwords.
  • Contact the appropriate authorities.
💳

I May Have Been Scammed

If you sent money or shared information with a suspicious person or service.

  • Stop further communication.
  • Contact your bank or payment provider.
  • Secure affected accounts.
  • Keep transaction information.
🛡️

My Personal Information Is Exposed

If your personal information has been shared or exposed online.

  • Identify what information was exposed.
  • Change affected passwords.
  • Review account privacy settings.
  • Monitor for suspicious activity.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I share my password when reporting an incident?
No. Never include your password, OTP, authentication code, recovery code, or private key in an incident report.
What should I include in an incident report?
Describe what happened, when it happened, and any relevant details that can help explain the incident. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information.
What should I do if I clicked a suspicious link?
Stop interacting with the website or message. If you entered credentials, change the affected password and review the account for suspicious activity.
Should I delete suspicious messages?
If the message may be useful as evidence, preserve screenshots or other relevant information before deleting or reporting it.
Can I report an incident through this website?
Yes. Use the Report Incident page to submit a cybersecurity incident associated with your account.
